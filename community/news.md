## New: Windows, To-Dos, Insights and Search by Meaning

- **Windows.** The app now runs on Windows too, with the same CF folder and the same Claude Code tools. Download it from github.com/Fun-Stuff-Shared/cognitive-fingerprint. Windows asks once before it runs a new app: press More info, then Run anyway.
- **To-Dos and Insights.** Turn them on in the Extraction Room. To-Dos lists what you promised on a call; Insights shows what a call showed about you that you did not say. Both read the calls you choose on your own Claude plan, and nothing goes to the CF service. See To-Dos and Insights in Help.
- **Search by meaning.** Find Your Roots finds what you meant as well as your words. Choose in setup or Settings: on your computer (a Mac with Apple silicon, or Windows), where the search model downloads once and no call text leaves it, or on the Cognitive Fingerprint server, which runs the same model on your own turns in the calls you choose and stores none of the text.
- **Connect Claude Code.** Press the agent pill on Home to add the CF tools to Claude Code in one step.

## Welcome to the Cognitive Fingerprint Lab

Your dashboard is live. Add your calls in the Extraction Room and press Find Your Roots; what they showed waits in Review. New Lab news shows up here when you press Sync on Home, and the app checks on its own every few hours.
