## New: Windows, To-Dos, Insights and Search on Your Computer

- **Windows.** The app now runs on Windows too, with the same CF folder and the same Claude Code tools. Download Cognitive-Fingerprint-Setup.exe from the latest release. Windows asks once before it runs a new app: press More info, then Run anyway.
- **To-Dos and Insights.** Turn them on in the Extraction Room. To-Dos lists what you promised on a call; Insights shows what a call showed about you that you did not say. Both read the calls you choose on your own Claude plan, and nothing goes to the CF service. See To-Dos and Insights in Help.
- **Search by meaning, on your computer.** Turn it on in Settings. On a Mac with Apple silicon and on Windows, the search model downloads once and runs on your computer, so no call text leaves it.
- **Connect Claude Code.** Press the agent pill on Home to add the CF tools to Claude Code in one step.

## Welcome to the Cognitive Fingerprint Lab

Your dashboard is live. Add your calls in the Extraction Room and press Find Your Roots; what they showed waits in Review. New Lab news shows up here when you press Sync on Home, and the app checks on its own every few hours.
